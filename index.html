mtre = (pred_pose(f) - true_pose(f)).norm(dim=-1).mean(dim=-1)

# pose maps camera → CT, so CT points need its inverse,
# as in DRR.perspective_projection
mtre = (pred_pose.inverse()(f) - true_pose.inverse()(f)).norm(dim=-1).mean(dim=-1)
  1. DiffDRR's pose places the camera in CT coordinates: Detector.forward applies it to the source and detector, and perspective_projection inverts it before projecting CT points.
  2. pose(f) reads the CT coordinates of the fiducials as camera coordinates. Those points (purple) lie around the X-ray source, which is the camera origin.
  3. Both point sets undergo the same rigid error. A translation moves both equally. A rotation about the anatomy moves each point by the angle times its distance to the rotation centre: about – mm for the fiducials and about – mm for the purple points.
